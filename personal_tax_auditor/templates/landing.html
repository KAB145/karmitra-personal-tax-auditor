How VAT Hides in Every Purchase

In Nepal, when you pay NPR 1,000 at a restaurant, you are not just paying for food. A portion of that amount is VAT collected on behalf of the government. KarMitra separates what you actually paid for goods versus what went to taxes.

// VAT is already embedded in the price you pay
total_paid = 1,000 NPR
vat_amount = 1,000 × 13 / 113 = 115.04 NPR
actual_price = 1,000 - 115.04 = 884.96 NPR

// On top of that, your salary also has income tax deducted
salary = 800,000 NPR/year
income_tax = 5,000 + 20,000 + 20,000 = 45,000 NPR
effective_rate = (45,000 + vat_total) / 800,000
